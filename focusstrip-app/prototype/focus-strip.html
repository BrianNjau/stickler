          How XP works

          1. Side quest +10 · Main quest +20 · Boss +30 per task
          2. +50 per block cleared, and +25 more if it was timed with zero drifts

          5. Rescue quests for neglected skills pay 1.5x · +2 per parked tangent
          6. +100 for clearing the whole day · +500 per certification
